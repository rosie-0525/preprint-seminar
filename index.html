Algebraic Geometry Preprint Seminar

Stanford University, Autumn 2026

When
Fridays, 3:00–4:00 pm
Where
Building 380, room 383N
Organizers
Weite Pi, Rosie Shen, Ziquan Zhuang

Each Friday one of us presents a recent arXiv preprint in algebraic geometry: what it proves, why it matters, and how the argument goes. The aim is a working understanding of the paper in an hour. Talks are informal, and everyone is welcome.
